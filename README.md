## Featured work

### 🧬 Identification and Elucidation of Therapeutic Targets in Nasopharyngeal Carcinoma
**Published, Current Trends in OMICS, 2026** | [DOI](https://doi.org/10.32350/cto.61.03)

Analyzed the GEO dataset GSE53819 (18 NPC tumors vs. 18 normal tissue) to identify differentially expressed genes and therapeutic targets. Used GEO2R, CPA (Wilcoxon, FGSEA, KS-test consensus), and STRING/Cytoscape with the CytoHubba MCC algorithm to build a protein-protein interaction network. Identified 10 hub genes and, for the first time, characterized them into two cooperating oncogenic modules: an inflammatory cytokine-chemokine axis (IL1B-CXCL8-CXCL12) and an ECM remodeling axis (FN1-COL1A1).


### 🧠 Computational Assessment of BMI Effects on Endocrine Signatures in PCOS
**MPhil thesis, in progress**

Applied Random Forest classification and clustering to a cross-sectional dataset of 508 women from fertility clinics in Kerala, India, to characterize how BMI relates to hormonal and reproductive markers in polycystic ovary syndrome. The Random Forest model reached an ROC-AUC of 0.927, compared to 0.769 for a lifestyle-only baseline. Right antral follicle count emerged as the top feature by importance, with a statistically supported lateralised right-ovary effect (p = 0.024).


### 🐍 In Silico Characterization of Snake Venom-Derived Anticancer Peptides
Used ESM-2 protein language model embeddings and K-means clustering to characterize venom-derived peptides, followed by Boltz-2 structural complex prediction against the cancer targets IGF2BP1 and P53, assessing their potential as anticancer candidates.
